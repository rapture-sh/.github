## Rapture

An independent software engineering studio in Zürich. We build software for other
companies, and we build products of our own and keep them running.

Most of what we write is private, in client repositories or in our own. What is public
sits here: the pieces that came out of our own products because they turned out to be
useful on their own, all MIT licensed.

We also made [kywi.ch](https://kywi.ch).

Open for projects. A few lines about what you have in mind is enough, and you get an
answer from a person: **hello@rapture.sh**, or [rapture.sh](https://rapture.sh).
